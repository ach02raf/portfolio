Testing that Prevents Incidents: A Practical Pyramid

Topics

01

Test the contract

Favor fast unit tests and contract tests at service boundaries. Mock only what you own.

02

Integration > E2E for most cases

A few critical E2E flows are enough. Focus on integration tests for realistic coverage with maintainable speed.

03

Data and determinism

Use seed data, freeze time, and isolate external effects. Flaky tests destroy trust and velocity.

04

Coverage as a guide

Track mutation coverage where possible and require thresholds in CI; quality over raw percentages.

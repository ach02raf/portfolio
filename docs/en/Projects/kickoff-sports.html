Kickoff Sports Platform

Kickoff Sports is a dual-platform sports facility management system connecting stadium owners with football enthusiasts. The platform consists of two specialized applications: a client app for players to discover and book sports fields, and a stadium management app for facility owners to manage their venues, schedules, and bookings. Built with modern web technologies, it streamlines the entire process from field discovery to reservation confirmation.

Main Features

✔ Dual-platform architecture: Client app for players and Stadium app for venue owners

✔ Real-time field availability and instant booking system

✔ Stadium management dashboard with booking calendar and revenue analytics

✔ Multi-field venue support with individual field scheduling

✔ Secure payment integration for online reservations

✔ Automated booking confirmations and notifications

✔ User account management with booking history

✔ Stadium profile customization with photos, amenities, and pricing

✔ Location-based search for nearby sports facilities

✔ Rating and review system for stadiums

✔ Responsive design optimized for desktop and mobile devices

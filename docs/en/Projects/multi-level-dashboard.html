Multi-level Organizational Dashboard

A multi-level organizational dashboard that centralizes information management and communication. Structured around a Super Administrator, each member organization has its own administrator, guaranteeing autonomy while remaining integrated within a global framework.

Main Features

✔ Centralized information management and inter-organizational communication

✔ Hierarchical structure with a Super Administrator and autonomous member organizations

✔ Complaint management

✔ Real-time discussion (chat/instant messaging)

✔ Event management

✔ User and role management

✔ Organization management

✔ Sharing and viewing reports via Power BI

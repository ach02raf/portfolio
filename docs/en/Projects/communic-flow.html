Communic Flow

Communic Flow is a multi-level organizational dashboard designed to modernize institutional communication. It connects organizations, their affiliates, and members through a centralized platform offering information management, role-based access control, event planning, claim management, real-time messaging, and Power BI reporting. Initially developed for the National Chamber of the Pharmaceutical Industry (CNIP), the application addresses real needs for coordination, transparency, and rapid information flow across complex ecosystems.

Main Features

✔ Centralized institutional information across organizations and affiliates

✔ Complete hierarchical structure: Super Administrator → Organizations → Members

✔ Advanced claim management: creation, tracking, and responses

✔ Real-time messaging with Socket.IO (groups and individual chats)

✔ Event planning and scheduling

✔ Advanced user, role, and permission management

✔ Management of organizations and their affiliates

✔ Power BI integration for interactive analytics and decision-making
